WHO WE ARE

Technology built around real manufacturing.

Larch Technologies is a technology company focused on helping businesses simplify and strengthen their manufacturing operations.

We understand that manufacturing is not just about producing a product. It involves planning, materials, machines, people, quality, inventory and delivery — all working together as one connected process.

Our approach is to understand how businesses actually operate and then use technology to bring their processes, information and teams together.

With a strong focus on automotive and manufacturing environments, Larch Technologies develops practical digital solutions that help organizations improve visibility and make better operational decisions.
