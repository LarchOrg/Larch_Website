Adopting IATF

One Integrated Platform for
An Automotive Manufacturers

Purpose-built modules engineered around the unique demands of Indian manufacturing and industrial operations — all in one integrated platform.

▶ JIT(Just in Time)

5S Inventory / SCM

5S Inventory & Supply Chain Management solution is designed to streamline manufacturing operations by combining 5S principles with advanced inventory control and end-to-end supply chain visibility. It enables organizations to maintain structured, efficient, and traceable workflows across the shop floor and supply chain.

Learn More →
▶ TPM(Total Productive Maintenance)

Maintenance / OEE

Plant Maintenance & OEE solution is an end-to-end asset and equipment management platform designed to maximize machine uptime, improve reliability, and enhance overall equipment effectiveness (OEE). It enables organizations to shift from reactive maintenance to predictive and data-driven strategies.

Learn More →
▶ APS(Advanced Planning and Scheduling)

Production Planning Control / MRP

Production Planning & Control solution enables manufacturers to efficiently plan, schedule, and execute production using advanced MRP and APS capabilities. It ensures optimal utilization of resources, improved delivery performance, and real-time visibility across the entire production lifecycle.

Learn More →
▶ QMS(Quality Management System)

Manufacturing Execution System / Quality IATF

A QMS is a complete digital quality management platform designed to ensure IATF 16949 compliance and streamline PPAP processes. It integrates supplier quality, SPC, audits, and compliance into one unified system.

Learn More →
▶ FMEA(Failure Modes and Effects Analysis)

MSA / SPC / Control Plan

Quality Intelligence Platform integrates MSA, SPC, and Control Plan into a single system to ensure measurement accuracy, process control, and IATF 16949 compliance across the shop floor.

Learn More →
▶ End to End Traceability

Traceability and Serialization

Traceability & Serialization module provides complete end-to-end visibility across the entire supply chain — from raw materials to finished goods delivery. It ensures full compliance, quality integration, and real-time production tracking.
