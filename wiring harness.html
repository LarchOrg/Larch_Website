WIRING HARNESS

From individual wires to complete assemblies.

Wiring harness manufacturing involves multiple stages, including wire cutting, stripping, crimping, routing, connector assembly, testing, and packing.

LarchERP brings these operations together with real-time production visibility, material control, quality monitoring, traceability, and manufacturing analytics.
